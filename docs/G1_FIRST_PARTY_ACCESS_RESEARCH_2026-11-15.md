### Observed access ≠ authority

The web research interface could retrieve several public pages and robots files, but it is not the deployed `AgentFuseResearchBot`, not proof of robots behavior for Zyte, and not an independent publisher consent workflow. Failure to retrieve a robots file through a research tool is **unknown**, not an automatic `Disallow`. Publisher licensing, contract scope and permitted fact reuse are separate questions.

The **highest-priority decision** is not choosing the easiest page to request; it is selecting a source with adequately substantiated rights *and* a source format that can produce reliable evidence with bounded maintenance. Supabase's published RSS option and GitHub's licensed source repository are concrete alternatives that may be better than Zyte HTML for those providers after review.
